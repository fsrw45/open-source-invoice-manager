# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in Open Source Invoice Manager a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give helpful and actionable feedback.
- Focus discussions on the project and its goals.
- Respect other contributors and community members.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any other behavior that creates an unsafe or unwelcoming environment.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainer privately through GitHub.

Please provide enough information to help us understand what happened.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove inappropriate content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a respectful project environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, community interactions, and other spaces related to the project.
